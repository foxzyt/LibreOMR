## How It Works

1. **Print**: Choose and print from dozens of ready-to-use answer sheets included with LibreOMR (10 to 100 questions, available in 6 languages).
2. **Scan**: Collect the filled sheets and scan them (or take clear photos) as PNG, JPEG, or BMP.
3. **Grade**: Open LibreOMR Desktop, enter your answer key, select your scans, and click **Grade**. Review instant scores, class statistics, and export results directly to Excel or CSV.

---

## Features for Teachers & Schools

- **Grade One or Hundreds of Sheets**: Process a single test or grade entire stacks of exam sheets in batch mode within seconds.
- **Ready-to-Print Answer Sheets**: Pre-designed A4 answer sheets (10, 20, 30, 40, 50, and 100 questions with 3 to 5 alternatives, plus economical 2-in-1, 4-in-1, and True/False layouts) in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, and Italian.
- **Custom Answer Keys & Point Weights**: Set correct answers, define specific weights for each question, customize the maximum grade scale, and easily void cancelled questions with automatic full credit.
- **Student Roster Mapping**: Import a class roster CSV with student names and IDs to match each graded sheet to a student automatically.
- **Interactive Verification**: Visually inspect student marks and confidence scores with highlighted overlays on every sheet.
- **Class Analytics**: Instantly see your class average, highest/lowest scores, pass rates, and an item-by-item difficulty breakdown to see which questions students missed most.
- **Easy Export**: Export full grade reports and question response summaries to CSV (ready for Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or school gradebooks) and JSON.
- **100% Private & Offline**: All processing happens entirely on your local computer. No student data or exam images are ever sent to the cloud.

## Download & Installation

1. Download the latest installer (`LibreOMR-Setup-1.2.0-x64.exe`) from the [**Releases Page**](https://github.com/foxzyt/LibreOMR/releases/latest).
2. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions.
3. Launch **LibreOMR Desktop** from your Start menu or desktop shortcut.

> **Windows Shell Integration**: You can also right-click any image or folder of scanned answer sheets in Windows Explorer and select **"Grade with LibreOMR"** to launch directly.
